Origins and location

Born in Belgium in 1996 I moved to Switzerland in 2010. Changes don’t scare me. To date, I’ve moved about twenty times. I have been a Swiss passport holder since 2020 and today I live in Renens to complete my Master’s degree, Digital Experience Design at ECAL.

Secondary education and German

Moving to a new country had an impact on my education espacially because I had to study new languages. During high school, I spent a year in St Gallen and passed the Goethe B1 certificate.

Arts and Sciences

In high school, I majored in Visual Arts yet I’ve always had very good marks in maths. In 2017, I took a career aptitude test with a psychologist. The results showed a perfect balance between my artistic and scientific interests. My minor subject was psychology.

Bachelor

After graduating high school (2017), I applied to EPFL to study computer science. I took some summer courses to catch up in some scientific subjects . But my health took a toll. So I dropped out after the first semester and focused on fitness for the rest of the school year. And even took the Fitness Instructor course with Fitspro to keep learning throughout this second semester.

Early 2018 I enrolled in biomedical studies and the HES health programme convinced that IT wasn’t for me. But an internship in the IT department of the City of Sion made me change my mind. I loved working with business IT specialists and felt at the right place. So I enrolled part-time at the Sierre University of Applied Sciences (HES) in Business IT and applied to work permanently at the City of Sion alongside my studies.

Developer and English

My level of English and German was quite basic when I left high school. I first gradually improved my English during course. Then I worked for two years as a research assistant, where I had to use English in European project meetings. I also taught courses in English. The ECAL DXD Master’s programme being also taught in English the practice never stops.

My priorities

I’ve always been passionate about my work, whether it was helping others when I was doing technical support for the City of Sion, or creating things through coding or design . But my health will always come first. I like working on things where I understand the purpose: why are we doing this? For whom? On what scale? This gives me a lot of motivation, and the work involved often leads to improvements that help achieve the objectives. This need for purpose is what led me to UX. To deliver the best possible product, you need to understand the users, their objectives and their motivations.

Working style

I’m a firm believer in agility. My working group at the HES used SCRUM on Tuleap, but the team struggled to get to work with it. So I pushed for and implemented a migration to Azure DevOps. I really liked that way of working. That said, I don’t think you need to use all the SCRUM tools. this must be tailored to each project. I enjoy working in a team and having my ideas challenged. Making decisions as a team (of technical people) allows us, in my view, to make the best decisions for long-term durability.

And outside of work?

As a child, I loved reading, brain teasers and drawing, and today I love puzzle or narrative video games. I love creating things; I even went through a cosplay phase. As I mentioned earlier, I like to keep up to date with healthcare, particularly mental health. But my passion these days is motorcycling. It’s the first time in my life that I’ve found an activity that allows my thoughts to focus. I love having a mind full of thoughts because it allows me to be creative, but it’s good to switch off sometimes. I also do track riding, even though it terrifies me. I’m stepping out of my comfort zone and improving my decision-making skills.
